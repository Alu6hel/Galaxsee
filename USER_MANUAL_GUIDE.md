# 📖 Galaxsee Pro — User Manual & Operational Guide

## 1. Quick Navigation & Shortcuts
- **Open Photo**: Click any photo thumbnail to open in an in-tab viewer or double-click to view fullscreen.
- **Pinch-to-Grid**: Use two fingers on touchscreens or the density slider (1-6 keys) to scale from 1 column up to 14 columns.
- **Marquee Selection**: Click and drag on any empty grid area to box-select multiple photos at once.
- **Compare Studio**: Select 2 to 4 photos and click **Compare** to inspect details side-by-side with locked zoom.
- **EXIF Inspector (Key I)**: View camera make, lens, exposure parameters, live RGB histogram, and GPS coordinates.
- **Edit Studio (Key E)**: Non-destructive exposure, contrast, temperature, 8-channel HSL color wheels, and tone curves.
- **Private Vault**: Secure photos behind PIN 1337 or custom biometric authentication with AES-256 encryption.

## 2. Supported Formats
- **Standard**: JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, TIFF, SVG
- **Camera RAW**: ARW (Sony), CR2/CR3 (Canon), NEF (Nikon), DNG (Adobe/Leica)
- **High Dynamic Range & Mobile**: HEIC/HEIF, Ultra HDR Gain Maps, Apple Live Photos
- **Video & Audio**: MP4, MOV, WebM, FLAC, WAV, MP3
